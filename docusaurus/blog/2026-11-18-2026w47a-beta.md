## 🔥 Major Changes

- Added comprehensive **Arc Flash Protection** configuration, including protection settings, TCC curves and support for IEEE 1584 Annexes I and H;
- Implemented **On-Load Tap Changer (OLTC)** voltage control for transformers, including automatic tap adjustment and configurable operating limits;
- Introduced a configurable **floating Elements Toolbar**, with horizontal, vertical and hidden modes;
- Added **voltage-dependent bus coloring** to improve visualization of voltage levels throughout the network;
- Introduced a **Grid and Snap-to-Grid system** for accurate positioning and alignment of network elements.

---

## ✔️ New Features and Improvements

- Added **TCC curve editor** with CSV import, clipboard paste and logarithmic visualization;
- Added arc flash protection configuration for buses, lines and transformers;
- Added visualization of bolted fault current (Ibf) for individual protection devices;
- Added visual **stability event indicators** directly on network elements;
- Improved transformer tap visualization through the Label Manager;
- Added configurable voltage-level color palettes;
- Improved transformer, generator and shunt positioning, including support for rotated elements;
- Added element-specific icons to toolbars, menus and graphical interfaces;
- Improved transformer OLTC parameter validation and discrete tap operation;
- Updated wxCrafter-generated interfaces and Portuguese translations;
- Updated Docusaurus to **v3.10**, preparing the documentation platform for v4.

---

## ⚡ Power System Analysis Improvements

- Improved OLTC integration into the Newton-Raphson power flow solver;
- Improved transformer tap handling and voltage regulation;
- Added complex impedance calculation and visualization, including resistance and reactance frequency-response curves;
- Improved short-circuit power calculations with support for different system power bases and units;
- Improved fault current calculations and preservation of electrical fault data;
- Improved electrical element alignment and connection geometry.

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

- Fixed transformer and generator positioning when restoring projects;
- Fixed connection node misalignment for rotated buses and electrical elements;
- Fixed OLTC tap limits and discrete tap position validation;
- Fixed fault current data handling in lines and transformers;
- Fixed several power flow and short-circuit calculation inconsistencies;
- Fixed TCC CSV import and decimal separator handling;
- Improved UI rendering, popup positioning and workspace interaction;
- Various minor stability, usability and interface fixes.
